# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want LucidMenu to be a welcoming, practical, and respectful open-source
community for contributors and users.

Participation should remain constructive regardless of experience level,
background, identity, or technical opinion.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and assume good faith where reasonable.
- Keep technical discussions focused on the project.
- Give useful, actionable feedback.
- Respect differing levels of experience.
- Credit other contributors when using their work.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Follow project rules concerning singleplayer and offline use.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or contribution workflows.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam or deceptive project promotion.
- Requests or contributions whose primary purpose is cheating against other
  players, interfering with GTA Online, bypassing anti-cheat systems, or
  compromising third-party accounts or services.

## Project Scope

LucidMenu is intended for GTA V Story Mode / offline singleplayer
experimentation. Community spaces should keep development and support within
that scope.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this Code of Conduct. Repeated or serious
violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project
community spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe behavior that violates this Code of Conduct,
report it privately to the project maintainers using an available private
contact channel.

Reports should be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible and
reviewed based on the available context and evidence.

## Attribution

This document is informed by common open-source community conduct practices
and adapted for the scope of the LucidMenu project.
